Meet Marcus

Marcus Chen
Founder & Principal Broker

Marcus Chen has been Chicago's most trusted real estate professional for nearly two decades. Born and raised in the Chicago suburbs, Marcus developed a deep love for the city's architecture, neighborhoods, and communities — a passion that drives every transaction he makes.

After earning his economics degree from the University of Illinois and his real estate license in 2006, Marcus quickly became one of Chicago's top-producing agents. He founded Marcus Chen Real Estate Group in 2012 with a simple mission: combine unmatched market expertise with technology to deliver exceptional results for every client.

Today, Marcus personally oversees every listing and buyer transaction, believing that real estate is too important to delegate. His negotiation skills, local knowledge, and data-driven approach have produced $45M+ in annual sales — and countless lifelong client relationships.

"Real estate isn't just transactions — it's people's lives, dreams, and futures. I take that seriously every single day."
